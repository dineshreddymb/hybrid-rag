Customer message:
Two of our sensors in the new section of the greenhouse go offline several times a day. The batteries are new. The other sensors are fine.

L1 notes:
Both nodes (named "Expedition 1" and "Expedition 2") are in a new section behind the cold store, about 70 m from the controller. E-312 is raised 4-6 times a day and clears after 10-20 minutes. Batteries are lithium, 98%. Signal strength in the app is around -112 dBm for both, which is at the edge.

The cold store is a steel-clad building, and it is between the controller and the nodes. Metal structures block the 868 MHz radio. The distance is also above the 60 m we plan for inside a greenhouse.

Recommended one SK-R1 repeater mounted on the greenhouse wall next to the cold store, with a line of sight to both the controller side and the new section. Customer ordered one SK-R1.

Update 2026-03-12:
Repeater installed and paired. Both nodes now route through the repeater, signal around -85 dBm. No E-312 since installation.

Resolution:
Radio path blocked by steel cold store plus distance. Solved with one SK-R1 repeater.
