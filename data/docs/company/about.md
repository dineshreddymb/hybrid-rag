# About Vellora Systems

## Company

Vellora Systems B.V. builds climate and irrigation control systems for commercial greenhouses. It was founded in
**March 2017** in Utrecht, the Netherlands, by **Maren Oduya** (CEO) and **Tobias Lindqvist** (CTO), two former
greenhouse service engineers. An engineering office in **Pune, India** opened in **2021**. As of October 2026 Vellora
employs **142 people** and has more than **1,900 installations in 23 countries** (September 2026).

Values: *Grow with evidence* (every setting should be explainable by a measurement), *Own the outcome* (whoever picks
up a customer problem owns it until it is fixed), and *Keep it serviceable* (standard connectors, documented Modbus RTU
protocol, replaceable parts).

## Products

| Product | What it is | Launched |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| G3 Controller (VG3-100) | Main controller that drives relays and irrigation valves | March 2022 |
| VX-8 Valve Expansion Module | Adds 8 valve outputs (V9–V16) to a G3 | March 2022 |
| SK-200 Sensor Node | Wireless climate sensor (temperature, humidity, CO2, light) | September 2022 |
| SP-10 Soil Probe | Wired substrate probe (moisture, EC, temperature) | September 2022 |
| SK-R1 Radio Repeater | Extends sensor radio range | May 2024 |
| Vellora Grow app | Mobile app for iOS and Android | 2.0 in April 2025 |
| Vellora Cloud API | REST API for integrations (current: v2) | v2 in June 2024 |

The older G2 controller receives security fixes until **31 December 2027**; the G1 is end of life.
