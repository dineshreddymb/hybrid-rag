# Expense and Travel Policy

## Claims and approvals

Owner: Finance (Lucia Ferreira). Submit claims in **ExpenseDesk** within **30 days**; claims older than 90 days are
not paid. A receipt is required for every expense over **€25**.

| Amount per claim | Approved by |
|------------------|-------------|
| Up to €500 | Line manager |
| €501 – €5,000 | VP or department head |
| Over €5,000 | CEO and Finance Controller |

## Meals, hotels and transport

Meals: **€35 per day** when travelling in your home country, **€55 per day** abroad; alcohol only at customer dinners.
Hotels: **€150 per night**, or **€210 per night** in higher-cost cities (Amsterdam, London, Paris, New York, Mumbai).
Private car: €0.23 per km. Home office: a one-time **€400** allowance every 3 years for staff working from home at
least two days a week.

## Travel

Book all travel in **Wayfare** at least 14 days ahead. Take the train for journeys under 4 hours. Flights under 6 hours
are economy; flights of **6 hours or more** may be premium economy; **business class needs written CEO approval**.
When visiting growers, follow their hygiene rules and never visit two different growers' greenhouses on the same day
without changing into clean clothes, to avoid spreading plant diseases.
