# IT and Information Security Policy

## Accounts and devices

Owner: IT Security (Dev Malhotra). Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and stored in the company password
manager. **MFA is required** on every account using an authenticator app or hardware key; SMS codes are not allowed.
Company laptops have full-disk encryption and must get updates within 14 days (critical updates within **72 hours**).
USB storage is blocked. Report a **lost or stolen device within 4 hours** to security@vellora.example or the IT hotline,
even outside office hours; IT will lock and wipe it remotely.

## Data, access and incidents

Customer data may only be stored in Vellora Cloud, the ticket system and the CRM, never on personal devices or
accounts. Secrets go in the secrets vault, never in code, tickets or chat. Access is reviewed **every quarter** by team
leads and removed on a leaver's last day. Production firmware can only be signed by the Firmware team lead and two
named deputies, using keys kept in a hardware security module. Report phishing with the Report phishing button and any
suspected incident to security@vellora.example straight away.
