# Leave Policy (2026 edition)

## Changes from the 2024 edition

Owner: HR (Aiko Tanaka). Effective **1 January 2026**, replacing the 2024 edition, for permanent employees in the
Netherlands and India.

| Topic | 2024 edition | 2026 edition |
|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Annual leave (full-time) | 18 days | **22 days** |
| Carry-over | 5 days, use by 30 June | **8 days, use by 31 March** |
| Self-certified sick days | 2 days | **3 days** |
| Parental leave, primary caregiver | 12 weeks | **16 weeks** |
| Parental leave, secondary caregiver | 4 weeks | **6 weeks** |
| Wellbeing days | none | **2 days per year** |

## Annual leave and wellbeing days

Full-time employees get **22 days** of paid annual leave per year plus public holidays; part-time employees get a
proportional amount. Up to 8 unused days carry over and expire on 31 March. Requests longer than 5 working days need
4 weeks' notice. Every employee also gets **2 wellbeing days** per year for rest and mental health: they can be taken
the same day, need no reason, and cannot be carried over.

## Sick leave and parental leave

Report sickness to your manager before 09:30; you may self-certify up to **3 days**, after which a doctor's note is
needed. **Parental leave** covers birth, adoption and surrogacy: **16 weeks** fully paid for the primary caregiver
(in at most two blocks) and **6 weeks** for the secondary caregiver, both within the first year. During the first
4 weeks back, parents may work 80 % of their hours for 100 % pay.
