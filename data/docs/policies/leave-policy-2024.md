# Leave Policy (2024 edition) — SUPERSEDED

## Status and rules

> **SUPERSEDED.** This policy applied from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025 and was replaced by the Leave Policy
> (2026 edition) on 1 January 2026. Keep it only for questions about leave taken in 2024 or 2025.

Full-time employees received **18 days** of annual leave. Up to **5 days** could be carried over and had to be used by
**30 June**. Employees could self-certify sickness for **2 days**. Parental leave was **12 weeks** for the primary
caregiver and **4 weeks** for the secondary caregiver.
